      一から届けるまでProcess

      How a project runs.

      1. BriefYou tell me what you want built, who it is for and when. I reply within a day.
      2. ScopeYou get a written scope and a fixed quote before any work starts.
      3. BuildWork arrives in milestones. Each one is a build you can open and try.
      4. ReviewYou test it and ask for changes. Nothing is released without your approval.
      5. HandoverThe release, the source code and handover notes are yours.

      Three ways to work together

      • Discovery sprintA written scope, a clickable prototype and a fixed quote. The smallest way to start.
      • Fixed-scope buildAn agreed product for an agreed price and date.
      • Ongoing engineeringMonthly capacity for a product that keeps growing.
