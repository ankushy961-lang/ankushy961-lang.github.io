A ₹0 business research toolkit that fact-checks before it builds

15 November 2026 · 4 min read

The toolkit is a zero-budget system that researches a business idea, has every claim checked by two independent checkers, validates the work with 14 tests and stops at an owner approval gate before anything is built. Ankush Yadav delivered it to a client; the product's name is withheld.

What problem does it solve?

New business ideas usually skip from a hunch to spending money. The research is thin, a few numbers are guessed, and by the time anyone checks them a logo, a domain and a few tools have already been paid for.

The toolkit reverses the order. Research comes first, every claim is checked, and nothing is built or bought until the owner has read the evidence and said yes. The budget for all of it is ₹0, with no paid services.

How does two-checker fact-checking work?

Each claim the research makes, such as a price range, a demand estimate or a capacity figure, is given to two checkers working independently. A claim survives only when both agree.

  • Both confirm: the claim stays in the report.
  • The checkers split: the claim is kept only as a marked estimate, or dropped.
  • Both refute: the claim is removed before the report is written.

The point is not that two checkers are always right. It is that a single unchecked number can no longer reach the owner looking like a fact.

What does the 14-test validator check?

Before a checkpoint is produced, a repository validator runs 14 tests. They confirm that the tracking files are present, that every claim had two checkers, that refuted claims are gone, that the budget is still ₹0 with no paid services, and that the skills the system relies on are valid.

If any test fails, there is no checkpoint. The validator would rather stop than produce a report with a known gap in it.

Why does everything stop at an owner gate?

The output of the research phase is a checkpoint report, a PDF that summarises the findings and lists the decisions the owner has to make. Phase 1 stays locked until the owner approves the checkpoint, and the later business phase stays locked after that.

That gate is the product. Software can gather evidence quickly; deciding to spend money is the owner's job, and the toolkit makes that decision explicit instead of implied.

Stack and scale. Node 22 and Python, a Chrome extension and a web workspace, with a brand system and three logo concepts. 14 of 14 validator tests passing, ₹0 budget.
