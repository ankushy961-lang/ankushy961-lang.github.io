Privacy

Privacy policy

Ankush Yadav, AI product developer, 2+ years · In effect from 16 November 2026

This site does not collect, store or sell personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no adverts and no tracking cookies. The project enquiry form sends nothing to a server and stores nothing: it only prepares an email in your own email app, which you then choose to send. If you email me, I use your message only to reply and to do the work you ask for.

Who is responsible?

This site is run by Ankush Yadav, an independent software developer based in India. For any privacy question or complaint, write to me using the email address in the Contact section. I am the contact for grievances.

What does the site collect when you visit?

Nothing that identifies you. The pages are static files. The site's own code is blocked from sending data anywhere, by a content security policy.

  • No cookies. The site sets none.
  • One setting on your device. If you pause the animations, your browser remembers that choice in its local storage. It stays on your device and is never sent to me.
  • Your time zone. The time-zone panel reads your device's clock to show your time next to mine. This happens in your browser and is not sent anywhere.
  • The demos. Every demo is a simulation on sample data. Nothing you click or type in a demo leaves your browser.

Which other companies are involved?

Loading any website shares your IP address and browser type with the servers that deliver it. For this site those are:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the site and keeps its own security logs.
  • Google Fonts, which delivers the typefaces.
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs), which delivers one open-source graphics library.

I do not receive those logs and cannot see who visited. Each company handles them under its own privacy policy. Their servers may be outside India.

What happens if you contact me?

If you email me, I receive your name, email address and whatever you write. I use it to reply, to scope and deliver the work, and to keep the records the law requires. I do not sell it, share it for advertising or add you to a mailing list. I keep it only as long as it is needed for those purposes.

If we work together, the handling of your business data is set out in our written agreement.

What are your rights?

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask me what personal data of yours I hold, ask me to correct or erase it, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to act for you. Write to me and I will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with my response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

This site is for businesses and professionals. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and I do not knowingly collect a child's data.

Changes

If the site starts to collect anything, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
