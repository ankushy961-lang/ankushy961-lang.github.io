The problem

A small retail operation has four kinds of people: the customer who orders, the shop that packs, the rider who delivers and the person who keeps it within the rules.

Most systems give each of them a different app and a server in the middle.

What we built

ShopConnect keeps all four in one Android build with no backend. Switching role changes the screens, not the data, so one order can move through every role on a single device without a network call.

Decisions that matter

  • No network calls: shops, products and orders live on the device.
  • Real checkout and real payment stay switched off.
  • Restricted items are locked, and the admin screen shows every flag.
  • The ordering rules are covered by automated tests.

About the demo

The demo uses fictional shops, people and prices. No real payment exists in it.
