Questions

Do you promise trading profits?

No. We build the software. Nothing on this site is investment advice, and we make no claim about trading performance.

How does the safety gate work?

The desk starts in PAPER mode, where no order leaves the machine. It must complete 50 paper trades at a 45 percent win rate or better before it is promoted to LIMITED LIVE. Full LIVE still needs an operator token.

The Strikedeck demo on this site is simulated: no broker connection and no orders sent.
