When this fits

Your users are on Android phones, often in places where the network drops.

You need the app to do real work on the device: read a paper with the camera, listen for a wake word, or keep taking orders offline.

What we build

  • Native Android apps in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.
  • Cross-platform apps in React Native and Expo, or web apps wrapped with Capacitor.
  • Offline-first data, so the app keeps working without a network.
  • Camera, voice, accessibility and foreground services where the product needs them.
  • A release you can install, with the source code and handover notes.

How we work

Seven steps from idea to handover: idea, strategy, design, development, testing, launch and handover. You get a written scope and a fixed quote before any work starts, and nothing is released without your approval.
