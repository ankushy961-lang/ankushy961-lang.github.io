When this fits

Paper arrives faster than anyone can type it in: slips, passes, invoices, forms.

The person holding the paper has a phone in one hand and no time for a form with twelve fields.

What we build

  • Capture with one action: point the camera and press once.
  • Detection of the document type before the fields are read.
  • A confidence level on every field, so people know what to check.
  • Matching to the right record by score, with the reasons shown.
  • A review queue for duplicates, decided by a person.

Seven steps from idea to handover: idea, strategy, design, development, testing, launch and handover. You get a written scope and a fixed quote before any work starts, and nothing is released without your approval.

Questions

What happens when the app cannot read a field?

It says so. Every field carries a confidence level, and a low reading is marked for a person to check. The app never pretends to know a value it half read.

Can it read handwriting and Hindi?

Sri Hari Docs works in Hindi and English. A handwritten name in fading ink reads with low confidence, so staff know to check it.
