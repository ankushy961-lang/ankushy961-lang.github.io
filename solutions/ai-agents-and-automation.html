Questions

How do you stop an AI agent from making mistakes?

We separate the jobs. In the research toolkit two independent checkers test every claim, and a refuted claim is removed before the report is written. Risky steps wait for a person.

Which AI models do you use?

Claude and Gemini in the cloud, and on-device models when the work should stay on the phone. Mikazuki uses two brains in the cloud and one on the device.
